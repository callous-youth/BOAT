# BOAT Code of Conduct

## Our Standards

BOAT is committed to providing a welcoming, respectful, and constructive community for everyone.

Examples of positive behavior include:

- Being respectful and considerate
- Providing constructive technical feedback
- Respecting different viewpoints and experiences
- Focusing discussions on improving the project

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behavior is not acceptable:

- Harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks
- Insulting, threatening, or disruptive comments
- Publishing another person's private information without permission
- Repeatedly disrupting Issues, Pull Requests, or Discussions

## Scope and Enforcement

This Code of Conduct applies to all BOAT community spaces, including GitHub Issues, Pull Requests, Discussions, and other project communication channels.

To report unacceptable behavior, contact the maintainers privately at **liuyaohua.918@gmail.com**.

Project maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate content and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary.
